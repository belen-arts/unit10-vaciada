VACIADA — LOS QUE QUEDAN

Can cloth hold what a community has lost?

Vaciada: Los que quedan is a series of woven textile objects structurally generated from the geographic data of depopulated Spanish towns. Spain has experienced one of the most severe rural depopulation crises in Europe. Entire communities have been erased, by civil war, by flooding, by state decree, by slow economic migration. What remains is data. The towns persist as information. The communities do not.

Each object is not designed, it is a direct physical consequence of its site. Street layouts determine folding and creasing. Elevation becomes density and weight. Nothing is decorative. Everything is a translation.

This tool is part of that project. It invites you to choose your own community anywhere in the world and translate its geography into a fold. Your fold joins a permanent archive alongside everyone else who has used this tool. You become the next maker.

HOW TO USE VACIADA
01
search a place

Type any community, town or village in the search bar. The map will navigate there.

02
draw a zone

Click "draw zone" then click two corners on the map to define your cloth rectangle.

03
draw fold lines

Click "draw lines" then click on the zone edge to place each end of a fold line. Lines run edge to edge.

04
assign mountain / valley

Each line shows M or V. Click to toggle. Red = mountain. Blue = valley.

05
simulate the fold

Use the slider on the right panel to fold the cloth. Drag to rotate the 3D view.

06
save your fold

Click "save fold", enter your name, community, and why you chose it. Pick a pin colour. Your fold joins the permanent archive.
