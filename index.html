HOW TO USE VACIADA
01
search a place

Type any community, town or village in the search bar. The map will navigate there.

02
draw a zone

Click "draw zone" then click two corners on the map to define your cloth rectangle. This is your piece of cloth.

03
draw fold lines

Click "draw lines" then click on the zone edge to place each end of a fold line. Lines must run edge to edge.

04
assign mountain / valley

Each line in the sidebar shows M or V. Click to toggle. Red = mountain fold. Blue = valley fold.

05
simulate the fold

Use the slider on the right panel to fold the cloth. Drag to rotate the 3D view.

06
save your fold

When you are done, click "save fold". Enter your name and why you chose this community. Your fold becomes part of the permanent archive.
